About ME

I became a pharmacist because I have always been fascinated by the way pharmacy brings the natural sciences together, and by how different substances affect the human body and support the healing process. Antimicrobial resistance threatens exactly this, causing some of our most important medicines to lose their effect. This, together with the idea that human, animal and environmental health are closely connected (One Health), led me to microbiology.

I do my research at the Helmholtz Institute for One Health (HIOH) in Greifswald, Germany. My research focuses on Gram-negative bacteria, mainly E. coli and K. pneumoniae in aquatic environments and the spread of antimicrobial resistance.

From 2027, I am looking for opportunities to use my background in pharmacy and microbiology to make a meaningful contribution in the pharmaceutical industry or research.
